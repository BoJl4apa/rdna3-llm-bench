# 2026-11 round: Qwen3.8-27B at Q8, Inkling 2 Mini, and a llama.cpp lane

This round asked two questions on one W7800 (gfx1100). Is Qwen3.8-27B better
at Q8 than at q4? And does Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini hold up against
it? Ollama cannot load Inkling on ROCm, since its support is Apple-silicon
only. So the harness gained a llama.cpp lane, and the same GGUF was run on both
engines to tie the two lanes together.

**TL;DR**

- **MTP at Q8 ties MTP at q4 on quality and decodes 9% faster** (55.6 against
  50.9 tok/s). It also needs 29.5 GiB at 32K context instead of 18.9 GiB, which
  costs it a shared card with a second ~21 GiB model.
- **ggml-org's Q8_0 GGUF of the same model scores 0.85 / 0.81.** It thinks
  longer than the library builds on both engines and runs into the token cap.
  Matching the library's sampling did not fix it.
- **Inkling 2 Mini runs on mainline llama.cpp ROCm** with coherent output:
  0.94 pass@1, but 26 tok/s and 0/5 on the batching scenario.
- **llama-server under HIP did not idle-spin** when run with
  `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1`.

## Conditions

This is the [2026-09](2026-09-round.md) harness, unchanged:

- HumanEval-100, official harness, sandboxed.
- Three agentic scenarios at n=5, with a 12-turn cap.
- Temperature 0.2, 16K context.
- Token caps: 3072 for HumanEval, 4096 for the agentic scenarios.

Inkling uses its vendor sampling instead: temperature 1.0, top-p 1.0, top-k 0,
min-p 0.

| lane | engine | card setup |
|---|---|---|
| Ollama | `ollama/ollama:0.40.3-rocm` | one card |
| llama.cpp | `ghcr.io/ggml-org/llama.cpp:server-rocm-b12648` | the same card; `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1`, `-ngl 99 -c 16384 --parallel 1 --jinja`, sampling set on the server |

No other LLM was loaded on the card during the llama.cpp arms; we polled
Ollama every 30 s. The usual quiet-box gate (no card above 40 W at start) was
waived for the llama.cpp arms. The Ollama arms passed it.

**How to read the table:**

- **Finished / capped:** passes over answers that ended under the 3072-token

  round is a capped answer.**
- **Decode:** the median over the 100 generations, from each engine's own
  counters.
- **Agentic columns:** `task_complete` runs out of 5. The scenarios are
  described on the [2026-09 page](2026-09-round.md).

## The ggml-org GGUF is not the library model

The ggml-org Q8_0, pulled through Ollama as `hf.co/ggml-org/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF:Q8_0`,
differs from the library tags in three ways besides the quant:

- It carries no sampling parameters, where the library tags ship top_k 20 and
  top_p 0.95.
- It has no `RENDERER`/`PARSER`, so it formats prompts with the GGUF's own
  template.
- Ollama does not fetch its separate MTP head, so it runs without MTP.

**It thinks more:** median thinking is 1,910 chars on Ollama and 2,245 on
llama.cpp, against 1,173–1,226 for the library builds. The tails run into the
cap.

**Sampling is not the cause.** The llama.cpp arm used the library's sampling
and still capped more often. That sampling is top_k 20, top_p 0.95, min_p 0,
and no repeat or presence penalty. The prompt template is what remains, and
we have not tested it.

**So these two rows measure the GGUF as shipped, not Q8 quality.** If you
compare quants across sources, diff `ollama show --modelfile` first.

**On the same file, the two engines decode at parity:** 19.5 tok/s on Ollama
against 20.0 on llama-server.

## MTP at Q8: faster, and the footprint is the cost

| build | VRAM at 32K context (sysfs) | with a 21 GiB second model loaded on the same 45 GiB card |
|---|---:|---|
| MTP q4_K_M | 18.9 GiB | both stay loaded (40.5 GiB) |
| MTP Q8_0 | 29.5 GiB | does not fit; Ollama evicts the Q8 |

**Why Q8 decodes faster than q4 under MTP is unknown.**

- Without MTP, the Q8 we have (the ggml-org file) is slower than q4: 19.5
  against 28.2 tok/s. That is what bandwidth-bound decode predicts, but it is a
  different file.
- Draft acceptance read from a shared engine log is about 0.77–0.79 for both
  builds. That is not reliable enough to explain the gap.

**The engine version moved both baselines.** On Ollama 0.40.3, MTP q4 runs at
50.9 tok/s and plain q4 at 28.2. On 0.33.2 the [2026-09 page](2026-09-qwen3.8.md)
measured 42.1 and 25.5. Compare across rounds only on the same engine.

## Inkling 2 Mini on gfx1100

It needs mainline llama.cpp b12611 or newer, and the Unsloth
`Mainline_Support` GGUF (rename `*.gguf_file` to `.gguf`). Older builds load
it, but the model produces wrong output.

- **Quality:** 0.94. All 92 finished answers pass, and 6 of 8 capped answers
  fail.
- **Rename 0/5.** Every run hit the 12-turn cap after editing 0–2 of the 12
  sites, at about two tool calls per turn. That is the serial-path failure from
  [two agentic modes](../findings/two-agentic-modes.md), not a lane artifact:
  the ggml-org Q8 on the same lane edited all 12 sites in two runs.
- **Speed:** 26.0 tok/s decode and about 640 tok/s prefill on an 8k-token
  prompt. That is no faster than the dense 27B, despite 3.4B active parameters
  (5.4B effective with its looped layers).
  - The file is mostly BF16: 330 BF16, 40 NVFP4 and 374 F32 tensors. All
    layers were on the GPU.
  - Slow BF16 matmul on RDNA3 under HIP is our suspicion, untested. A Vulkan
    run or a non-BF16 quant would settle it.

## Engine notes

**llama-server HIP with one hardware queue did not idle-spin.** With
`GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1`, Inkling loaded on the card idled at 15–26 W and opened 2
KFD compute queues. Before the load, the card read 17–19 W with only a small
reranker resident. The samples cover about 2.5 minutes, before and after
inference, for one model on one build.
- Compare the 80–87 W spin in [resident-model idle power](../findings/resident-model-idle-power-vulkan-vs-hip.md),
  measured uncapped on an older build with a dense 8B.
- **Treat this as a pointer, not a requalification.** Build, model and the cap
  all changed at once.

**llama-server rejects malformed tool calls.** When the model emits tool-call
arguments that are not valid JSON, llama-server answers HTTP 500
(`Failed to parse tool call arguments as JSON`). A harness must count that as a
model error, not retry it as a transport failure. It ended two of the ggml-org
Q8's rename runs on llama.cpp.
